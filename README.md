## System Intent & Core Governance Principles

GMADSS is a generalized, read-only, advisory-only decision-support architecture designed to enhance policy fidelity, structural documentation clarity, and operational compliance across human-serving, regulatory, and public-sector environments.

The architecture is fundamentally decoupled from any single proprietary IT system or provincial database. It functions as an open epistemic layer that synthesizes authoritative source material—legislation, operational directives, systemic review evidence—without automating outcomes, generating scores, or bypassing human accountability.

GMADSS enforces the following structural governance invariants:

**Strict Advisory Posture**  
GMADSS does not automate decisions, generate triage scores, or execute operational commands. It preserves full human accountability at all times.

**Anti-Narrative Smoothing**  
The system is engineered to expose contradictions, statutory friction, and conflicting data rather than harmonizing or smoothing them.

**Preservation of Uncertainty**  
Where evidence is incomplete or ambiguous, GMADSS preserves uncertainty and explicitly surfaces it to human oversight operators.
